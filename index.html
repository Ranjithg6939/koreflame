The KoreFlame Standard

Signature Smokehouse Highlights

Four culinary traditions that elevate our authentic Seoul tabletop barbecue above ordinary smokehouses.

100% White Oak Binchotan

Kiln-fired Japanese white oak burns past 800°F with pure infrared rays, sealing juices instantly with zero chemical soot.

48-Hour Pear Marinade

Fresh sweet Asian pear, tamari soy, whole smashed garlic, and wild honey naturally tenderize without meat tenderizers.

Smokeless Downdraft

Commercial-grade underground suction pulls 99% of grease and smoke beneath the floor so your clothes stay completely fresh.

Artisan Living Banchan

8 probiotic fermented sides crafted fresh at 6:00 AM daily, paired with endless complimentary refills for every guest.
