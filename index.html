The KoreFlame Standard

Signature Smokehouse Highlights

Four culinary traditions that elevate our authentic Seoul tabletop barbecue above ordinary smokehouses.

100% White Oak Binchotan

Kiln-fired Japanese white oak burns past 800°F with pure infrared rays, sealing juices instantly with zero chemical soot.

48-Hour Pear Marinade

Fresh sweet Asian pear, tamari soy, whole smashed garlic, and wild honey naturally tenderize without meat tenderizers.

Smokeless Downdraft

Commercial-grade underground suction pulls 99% of grease and smoke beneath the floor so your clothes stay completely fresh.

Artisan Living Banchan

8 probiotic fermented sides crafted fresh at 6:00 AM daily, paired with endless complimentary refills for every guest.

Tabletop Sizzle & Ssam Ritual

The Live Table Grill Experience

More than just dining — Korean BBQ at KoreFlame is an interactive, sizzling performance where you control the heat, slice sizzling prime galbi with table shears, and fold fresh wraps at your own leisurely pace.

Precision Table Shears & Tongs Our grill captains provide ergonomic Korean kitchen shears and high-temp tongs, cutting through thick galbi strips effortlessly over the sizzling grate.
The Traditional Ssam Wrapping Ritual Stack red leaf lettuce and serrated perilla leaves, place grilled meat in the center, and crown with aged savory dip, grilled garlic, and scallion slaw for a single-bite burst.
Zero Clothes Smoke Guarantee Enjoy deep charcoal flavor without smelling like barbecue afterward. In-table downdraft exhausts pull vapor straight down before it rises into the air.
