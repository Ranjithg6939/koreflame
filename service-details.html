VIP Private Dining Suites

Exclusivity, privacy, and Michelin-caliber Korean BBQ hospitality. Designed for intimate anniversaries, high-stakes business dinners, and family milestones.

The Ultimate Private Seoul Sizzle Experience

Step behind our hand-carved charred cedar sliding doors into an acoustically shielded dining sanctuary. Our VIP suites feature sunken Hinoki-finished dining tables fitted with subterranean Japanese smokeless downdraft grills, ensuring an ambiance completely free of residual cooking smoke.

Exclusive Suite Inclusions

Dedicated Head Grillmaster A dedicated chef to carve BMS 9+ Wagyu and Prime Galbi right in front of you.
Private Sommelier & Mixologist Bespoke soju flights, Korean plum wines, and curated temperature pairings.
Custom Audio & Visuals Independent Bluetooth sound systems and presentation-ready 4K display screens.
Reserve Banchan Cellar Access Tastings of rare aged 5-year mukeunji kimchi and wild mountain root namul.
