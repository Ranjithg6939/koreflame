Reservation Policy & FAQs

How long is our table held?

We hold confirmed reservations for up to 15 minutes past your booking time before releasing the grill to waiting walk-in guests.

What is the dining duration?

Parties of 1-4 are allotted 90 minutes; parties of 5+ or VIP private suites are allotted 120 minutes of leisurely table grilling.

Can we modify or cancel later?

Yes! Use your reference code in the Guest Dashboard to modify your time or cancel anytime up to 2 hours prior.

Do downdraft tables really prevent smoke?

Yes. Our Japanese-engineered subterranean downdraft system extracts smoke at the grill perimeter before it can rise into the air.
