Our Story & Traditions

The Soul Behind KoreFlame

Born from the vibrant alleyways of Mapo-gu in Seoul, KoreFlame celebrates the raw energy of sizzling charcoal, hand-cut prime meats, and generational fermentation.

Founding Philosophy

Where Street Energy Meets Culinary Precision

In 2024, Executive Chef Min-Jun Kang envisioned a restaurant that captured the authentic warmth of late-night Seoul grill tents (Pojangmacha) combined with pristine, modern Japanese downdraft engineering and artisanal butcher cuts.

Too often, Western Korean BBQ relies on gas burners and sugary artificial sauces. At KoreFlame, every single ember is pure Kishu white oak Binchotan. Every marinade rests for 48 hours in blended Korean sweet pears, toasted sesame, and raw garlic.

Our Pillars

The Four Tenets of Our Craft

We hold ourselves to uncompromising standards of sourcing, fire, and fermentation.
