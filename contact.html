Good to Know

Guest Inquiries & Dining Policies

Helpful answers to common questions regarding reservations, parking, allergies, and dining rules.

Walk-in Table Policy

We hold 30% of dining floor capacity for walk-ins every evening. However, during peak weekend hours (6:30 PM – 9:00 PM), advance reservations via our Packages page are strongly recommended.

Valet Parking & Transit

Complimentary valet drop-off is stationed directly in front of our main entrance on 5th Avenue from 5:00 PM onward. We are also a 3-minute stroll from 14th St and Times Square subway hubs.

Dietary & Halal Protocol

We provide clean grates, certified Halal Wagyu cuts, and vegetarian/vegan side dishes. Simply notify your server upon seating or mention it in your booking request.

Corkage & Cake Policy

Corkage is $35 per 750ml bottle (max 2 bottles per table). Celebratory birthday cakes may be brought in with zero cutting fee; our staff gladly provides sparkling table celebration service.
